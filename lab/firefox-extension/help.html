rocket_launchQuick Start

  1. Open a supported inbox page and select an email so the full message body is visible.
  2. Open the toolbar popup and choose Open In-Page Helper.
  3. Use the helper tabs to review the email mirror, workflow output, and diagnostics.
  4. Use the copy actions when you want final URLs, digest output, rewritten content, or diagnostics on the clipboard.

settingsSettings

URL normalization and repair

Default: off. When enabled, detected URLs are normalized and repaired before mirror content and final outputs are generated.

Replace opened email body with mirror content

Default: off. When enabled, the opened email body is replaced in-page with the current mirror content after the snapshot is ready.

Auto-apply mirror for configured sender addresses

Default: off. This applies mirror replacement automatically for messages that match the sender address list managed on the settings page.

visibilityWhat The Helper Shows

Email Mirror

A formatted mirror of the opened message so you can inspect links in context before interacting with them.

Workflow

The step-by-step URL pipeline, including raw detection, cleanup, resolution details, final URLs, digest output, and modified email content.

Diagnostics

Detection mode, counts, and any pipeline errors that can explain why a message did or did not populate correctly. Storage values now live on the Storage page from Settings.

supportTroubleshooting

No email detected

Make sure an email is open in the reading view, not just the inbox list. The helper stays empty until the message body is visible and readable on a supported host.

The popup says the helper is unavailable

Check that the current tab is one of the supported webmail hosts and that the page has finished loading. Then reopen the popup and refresh diagnostics.

The wrong message stays in the rail after navigation

Use the helper Refresh button after major inbox navigation changes. If the issue persists, reopen the target email and then open the helper again.

Copy actions do not produce the expected output

Check the Diagnostics tab for pipeline errors, then confirm whether URL normalization and repair is on or off. Those settings change the final output.
