settingsExtension Settings

Control URL cleanup, in-page mirror replacement, and sender-based auto-apply behavior.

URL normalization and repair When on, detected URLs are normalized, repaired, and used in mirror content. Default: off. Warning: URL repair can make a dead or broken dangerous link become active again.
Replace opened email body with mirror content When on, the opened email body is replaced in-page with the current mirror content as soon as the snapshot is ready. Default: off.
Auto-apply mirror for configured sender addresses When on, the extension automatically replaces the opened email body with mirror content for messages from the configured sender address list. Default: off. Configured list: loading…

alternate_emailConfigured Sender Addresses

Add or remove the sender email addresses that trigger automatic mirror replacement when the generic auto-apply setting is enabled.

Matching is based on sender email addresses, not display names. Mixed case is normalized automatically, and duplicate addresses are ignored.
